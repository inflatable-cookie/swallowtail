## Intent and boundary

Turn the broad pre-release g01 implementation into a deliberately versioned,
packageable, testable library surface that consumers upgrade without tracking
every provider release. Explicitly not API 1.0, not crates.io publication,
not a freeze on evidence-led provider work. Boundary: 49 roadmaps
(48 completed, one backlog move); 24 workspace crates at `0.1.0`.

## Shipped outcomes

- **Release boundary** — public crate, version, MSRV, release-authority, and
  compatibility boundaries; reproducible packaging without publishing.
- **Prepared integration layer** — contract-bound prepared facades replacing
  low-level-only consumer assembly, proved across installed, hosted-direct,
  and attached-runtime shapes and every remaining route family.
- **Consumer adoption** — Nucleus and Soundcheck migrated under consumer
  authority; packaged cross-consumer runtime proof; canonical-history final
  candidate with sustained normal-path workload hardening.
- **Session lifecycle** — consumer thread lifecycle separated from bound
  provider-session management; Codex archive/restore/delete, ACP close/delete
  with Claude Agent proof, OpenCode deletion, Kimi local-server route, and
  source-scoped model catalogue coverage.
- **Feature-matrix closure** — 660-cell region closed at 202 `Yes`,
  234 classified `No`, 216 `Not applicable` (plus Partial/caller-supplied/
  session-negotiated cells); session-continuity tranche without flattening
  replay, reattachment, or native close.
- **Observable activity and topology** — provider-visible agent activity via
  exact route profiles, child-work topology acceptance, and Claude form
  elicitation through typed harness input.
- **Structural and validation health** — error-level debt removed (doctor to
  warning-only), focused/affected-package validation selectors with frozen
  evidence-tier budgets.
