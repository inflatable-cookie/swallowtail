# Cursor And Antigravity Route Refocus

Date: 2026-07-31

## Outcome

Research 075 selected Cursor as the next installed-harness implementation and
Antigravity as the following Google personal-account route.

Gemini support remains in production, but its `0.53.0` maintenance tranche is
paused. The operator's authenticated no-response result and Google's migration
of personal OAuth access make more Gemini qualification low value for the
current use case. Enterprise and paid API-key users remain valid reasons to
retain the adapter.

## Cursor Shape

One new Cursor integration family will expose separate catalogue, ACP, and
headless drivers behind `prepare_cursor`. Automatic executable discovery will
prefer `cursor-agent`; the generic `agent` name is Grok on this host and cannot
be a safe automatic candidate.

The installed `2026.07.01-41b2de7` build and current ACP-registry build are
separate exact qualification points. No continuous calendar-version range is
assumed.

## Antigravity Shape

Antigravity remains separate from Gemini. Its documented model catalogue,
stream-JSON headless execution, structured output, activity, usage, subagent,
and conversation-id surfaces justify an adapter after exact artifact
reconciliation and installation.

Its optional sandbox stays opt-in. Its dangerous permission-bypass mode is out
of scope.

## Evidence Boundary

Local checks observed executable identity, versions, help, account posture,
and Cursor model listing only. No provider prompt, inference, session creation,
workspace mutation, consumer edit, or publication ran. No account identity or
raw provider payload was retained.
