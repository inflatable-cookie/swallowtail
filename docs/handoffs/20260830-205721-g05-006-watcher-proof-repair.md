prove Stop re-entry, emitted terminal-only activity through an adapter-local

repair lane. No transcript or second prompt is part of the authority chain.

## Why It Matters

A consumer must see a watcher while it is running, not only after it finishes.
A future live proof must also distinguish a real active Stop block and
same-session continuation from proactive model waiting or adapter-only terminal
rejection. Without both, Swallowtail still cannot claim dependable watcher
support.
