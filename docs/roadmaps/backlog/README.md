## Promotion Rules

- its stated evidence or authority gate is satisfied
- current contracts support a bounded roadmap
- the active generation has capacity and the item matches its programme
- the operator approves any product-policy or credential-bound choice

Backlog items are not ready tasks. Their source roadmaps and cards remain
historical evidence.
