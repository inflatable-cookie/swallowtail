## Goal

Freeze current exact-model and Live-facade `thinkingLevel` behavior, then
define the smallest Gemini Live reasoning-selection subset that can satisfy
Contracts 027, 037, and 040.

## Method

1. Freeze the current official model page and Live API capability/setup
   documentation for exact model `gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview`, the `v1beta`
   `BidiGenerateContent` surface, `thinkingConfig.thinkingLevel`, and every
   documented value. Record retrieval dates and stable source references.
2. Freeze current route source and deterministic fixtures for model, endpoint,
   facade axis/point, private behavior revision, initial setup, resume setup,
   prepared input, plan, request, driver validation, and fresh restoration.
3. Confirm or reject each candidate mapping independently:
   `minimal→MINIMAL`, `low→LOW`, `medium→MEDIUM`, and `high→HIGH`. Do not infer
   `off`, `default`, `xhigh`, `max`, numeric budgets, or aliases.
4. Prove the omission boundary. Current initial and resume fixtures send
   `MINIMAL`; omission must continue to send those exact bytes while remaining
   distinct from an explicit planned `minimal` selection.
5. Prove whether one selected level is valid and immutable across initial
   setup, provider-planned rollover/resume setup, and fresh realtime
   working-state restoration. Do not turn rollover into retry or public resume.
6. Classify the exact model/facade applicability and decide the new opaque
   facade point and adapter-private behavior revision. Do not rewrite the
   current behavior or extend another version/model by inference.
7. Classify evidence truth under Contract 040: dispatch is knowable from
   deterministic setup bytes; provider acceptance, effectiveness, and thought
   summary disclosure remain separate unless this exact surface returns
   explicit confirmation.
8. Replace Research 193's reservation with exact route/value dispositions and
   a deliver-now table. Do not edit shared architecture, matrices, programme,
   indexes, changelog, or roadmap front doors.

No credential, account inspection, provider request, paid operation, browser
login, or live Gemini call is authorized. Current official documentation and
secret-free repository/source inspection are sufficient for this gate.
