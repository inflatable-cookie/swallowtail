## Scope

1. Add deterministic prepared-facade and protocol coverage for every Research
   196 deliver-now value/profile and rejected boundary class.
2. Assert exact model, facade, private behavior, plan/evidence, driver, create
   request, and any admitted returned-tier observation.

5. Prove only Research 196-admitted detachment and restart-reconciliation paths;
   reject or withhold the rest without weakening their current behavior.
6. Prove service tier composes with absent and every admitted reasoning value
   plus absent and representative structured-output schemas.
7. Prove alias, unknown value, model/facade/plan/evidence drift, missing or
   mismatched returned tier, unqualified profile, and malformed response
   behavior exactly as Research 196 specifies.
8. Update the OpenAI Background prepared guide, Research 196, cards 136-138,
   g04.049, reserved route-local closeout, examples, and package-specific
   unreleased API baseline when applicable.
9. Record the exact architecture, route/feature matrix, programme, indexes,
   changelog, Contract 029, and Next Task delta in the closeout and PR body.
   Do not edit those shared surfaces on the worker branch.

  reconciliation, lifecycle, or cleanup truth cannot be proved
- docs would infer provider acceptance, access, pricing, latency, or capacity

  scope

## Out Of Scope

  later feature selection

## Closeout

explicit `default` evidence/driver/wire agreement, composition with absent and
every admitted reasoning value plus structured output, alias/unknown rejection,
detachment rejection, selected-tier checkpoint rejection before network,
ignored returned `service_tier`, and preserved reattachment/cancel/delete.

this closeout, and the unreleased OpenAI public-api baseline record the
dispatch-only subset. Shared indexes, architecture, CHANGELOG, and Next Task
